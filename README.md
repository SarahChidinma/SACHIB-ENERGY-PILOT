# SACHIB ENERGY (SACHIB-CENERGY)
### Intelligent Church Energy Solution
> *“Light in every dwelling.”* — Exodus 10:23

**SACHIB Energy** is an AI-powered energy intelligence and remote-monitoring platform designed to help churches, church-owned institutions, businesses, families, and members manage solar energy systems more reliably and affordably. 

The platform monitors, predicts, and optimizes energy availability and usage, eliminating uncertainty and reducing reliance on expensive diesel/petrol generators and erratic grid power.

## ⚡ Core Capabilities
- **Real-Time Monitoring:** Track solar generation, battery state of charge (SoC), consumption, and power sources live.
- **Predictive Intelligence:** AI-driven forecasting for solar generation and runtime based on load patterns and weather.
- **Prescriptive Advisories:** Actionable recommendations (e.g., shed heavy non-essential loads before cloudy weather).
- **Proactive Alerts:** Early warnings for critical battery levels, equipment anomalies, and unexpected surges.
- **Remote Access:** Total system visibility from any smartphone or browser.
